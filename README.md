# TreasuryOS

**A mini treasury management system (TMS) for a fictional French group, built from bank file to cash decision.**
*Un mini système de gestion de trésorerie pour un groupe français fictif, du relevé bancaire à la décision de trésorerie.*

**Live app:** [treasuryos-lumen.netlify.app](https://treasuryos-lumen.netlify.app/) · English and French · Author: [Saranne Ndamba](https://saranne-ndamba-portfolio.netlify.app/)

> All data is synthetic. Groupe Lumen, its companies, banks, accounts, people and figures are fictional and generated by the scripts in this repository. No real company data is used.

---

## The group

**Groupe Lumen** is a mid-sized French group headquartered in Paris.

| Company | Country | Currency | Banks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lumen SA (parent) | France | EUR, USD | BNP Paribas, Société Générale, HSBC |
| Lumen Deutschland GmbH | Germany | EUR | BNP Paribas |
| Lumen UK Ltd | United Kingdom | GBP | HSBC |
| Lumen Maroc SARL | Morocco | MAD | Société Générale Maroc |

Six bank accounts, a EUR zero-balancing cash pool at BNP Paribas, and a EUR 25m syndicated revolving credit facility (RCF) with three covenants.

## Modules

| # | Module (EN / FR) | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bank connectivity / Connectivité bancaire | Parses ISO 20022 **camt.053** and SWIFT **MT940** statements, checks that every file reconciles (opening + movements = closing), categorises each line with 17 priority rules, builds the daily multi-bank, multi-currency cash position in EUR | Live |
| 2 | Cash forecast / Prévisionnel de trésorerie | 13-week rolling forecast by company and category (direct method: receivables × payment probability, payables, run-rates, known calendar), 12-month driver-based forecast, forecast vs actual with accuracy (WAPE) and cause of each miss, reforecast scenarios | Live |
| 3 | Cash pooling / Centralisation de trésorerie | Reads the zero-balancing sweeps from the statements, turns them into intercompany loan positions, computes monthly arm's length interest (ACT/360), segments cash into operating buffer, reserve and surplus, proposes investments within the treasury policy | Live |
| 4 | Bank relationships / Relations bancaires | Account and signatory register, monthly bank fee audit against the tariff, pricing against a market benchmark, share of wallet against credit share, RCF usage and cost, covenant tracking, annual bank review scorecard and compliance calendar | Live |
| 5 | Payments / Paiements | SEPA **pain.001** payment file from a payment run, with a four-eyes approval workflow | Next |
| 6 | FX hedging / Couverture de change | Net FX exposure by currency, forward hedging proposal, P&L impact | Planned |
| 7 | KPIs / Indicateurs | Cash conversion cycle, DSO, DPO, forecast accuracy, liquidity headroom, bank fees as % of flows | Planned |

## Key results on the sample data (as of 6 Nov 2026)

- **665 bank lines** parsed from 6 files across 3 banks; all 6 files reconcile; **663 of 665** lines categorised correctly (the 2 misses are left for manual review on purpose).
- **Group cash: EUR 12.1m.** 13-week low point EUR 9.7m in the week of 28 Dec (13th-month salaries and year-end); liquidity headroom EUR 22.7m.
- **Forecast accuracy 93.5%** (1 − WAPE) against the forecast made on 7 Aug.
- **Cash pool:** Lumen Deutschland lends EUR 3.8m to Lumen SA through the pool; EUR 17.4k intercompany interest since 10 Aug. **EUR 3.7m surplus** available to invest within policy, earning about EUR 18k over 3 months.
- **Banks:** EUR 19k fees a year, EUR 4.1k saving at market pricing, 3 invoices flagged for query. HSBC earns 20% of the group's banking wallet against a 25% share of the RCF. All three covenants met.

## Standards and concepts covered

- **ISO 20022:** camt.053.001.08 (end-of-day statement), balances OPBD / CLBD, bank transaction codes (Domain-Family-SubFamily, e.g. `PMNT-RCDT-ESCT`, `CAMT-ACCB-ZABA`), EndToEndId; camt.052, camt.054, camt.086, pain.001, pain.002 in the glossary.
- **SWIFT MT940:** tags `:20: :25: :28C: :60F: :61: :86: :62F: :64:`.
- **French banking:** EBICS T / TS, CFONB 120, convention de trésorerie, article L511-7 of the Code monétaire et financier.
- **SEPA:** SCT, SCT Inst, SDD Core and B2B, Verification of Payee (VoP).
- **Treasury:** zero balancing (remontée / descente), arm's length interest, cash segmentation, liquidity headroom, share of wallet, covenants (leverage, interest cover, minimum liquidity).

A bilingual glossary of 42 terms is in the app (Glossary tab) and in [`LEXIQUE.md`](LEXIQUE.md).

## Run it locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```

To regenerate the data from scratch:

```bash
python3 generate_data.py
python3 make_statements.py
python3 make_forecast_inputs.py
```

The generators use a fixed random seed, so the output is identical every run. The camt.053 files were validated with strict parsing against the camt.053.001.08 schema (pyiso20022).

You can also drop your own camt.053 or MT940 file onto the Bank connectivity tab: it is parsed in the browser and never leaves your computer.
